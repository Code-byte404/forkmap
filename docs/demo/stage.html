I'm building a habit tracker as a side project. What should I build the backend with?

Here are four solid options for the data layer:

  1. PostgreSQL. Postgres is the safe default for relational data, and streaks and check-ins are very relational.
  2. SQLite. SQLite is the fastest way to a working prototype: one file, zero setup, and it runs on the phone too.
  3. MongoDB. MongoDB if the habit schema keeps changing and you'd rather not write migrations.
  4. Supabase. Supabase gives you Postgres plus auth out of the box, hosted for you.
Not Mongo, streak queries need joins. Keep Supabase in mind if hosting gets annoying. Let's go with Postgres.

Ok Postgres. Which ORM?

Three ORMs fit well with Postgres and TypeScript:

  1. Drizzle. Drizzle stays close to SQL and has a tiny runtime.
  2. Prisma. Prisma has the smoothest schema workflow, at the cost of a heavier client.
  3. Kysely. Kysely if you just want typed SQL without an ORM.

How should I handle auth?

You have three realistic paths for auth:

  1. Magic links. Magic links keep signup to one field.
  2. OAuth. Google and Apple sign-in; Sign in with Apple is required on iOS if you offer other social logins.
  3. Email and password. Classic email and password is the most familiar, but you own resets.
